Penuel International Pentecostal Church

Our Story

Penuel church was established in approximately 2012 by Apostle Habirangi Bolingo Moses who had the vision. a person who had the idea to establish a church in Kampala and wanted to spread that concept globally by planting churches in various locations. That why he began with the church in Kampala, then move on to South Sudan, Kenya, and eventually Congo DRC. He aimed to share the positive message of Jesus Christ and rescue souls lost in sin. Church of Penuel guides individuals in living a prayerful life and leading others to the kingdom of heaven.

  • Individuals receive baptism at Penuel church.
  • We offer instructions, workshops, and even organizations.
  • We also have prayers scheduled every Saturday between 9am and 12pm.

Shepherds are present in all of our church locations, including Kampala, South Sudan, Kenya, and Congo DRC.
