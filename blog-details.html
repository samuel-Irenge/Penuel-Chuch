The life of Christians at Penuel International Penteconstal Church.

Penuel church teaches Christians how to live in love, how to love one's neighbor and share together, come in agreement with God, and live your life in prayer and repentance. That is why the men of God never cease to show us the way to walk with the Lord, avoiding Satan's traps.

The Life of a Christian.

A Christian lives by principles guided by the level of his or her faith and commitment to God. In this way, the commitment to perform God's commandments forms the basis of an individual's life to be called a true Christian. This incorporates love toward one's neighbor, forgiveness, humility, and respect for others. Such values need to be applied practically by means of assisting others, honesty, and serving society. A Christian is supposed to foster a personal relationship with God through prayers and meditation on the Scriptures. One is supposed to pray so as to discern the action of the Holy Spirit in his life. While he strives to live his life in accordance to biblical precepts, he is encouraged to exercise virtues like meekness, chastity, and patience.

Penuel Church members.

The servants of Penuel Church Uganga-Kamapala should remain with a personal relationship with God through frequent prayers and scripture reading. These acts of spiritual nature provide grounds for allowing their faith and understanding of God's will to grow. Participation in the community-ecclesial is a basic element that consists of presence at worship, Bible studies, and community service. Very often they and are called to support the initiatives of the Church and attend to the works of charity as an expression of love of neighbour.

It is from the way a pastor or spiritual leader lives his life that impacts many members of the church. When leaders live a life that models God's Word and demonstrates a serious commitment to community, the members can be likewise inspired to do the same. On the other hand, the wrong behaviors of leaders can also lower the level of spirituality in the members.
